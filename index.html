Main things im doing

I co-founded Maize with Om and Yousef. A big problem we're seeing in agriculture is the lack of affordable crop-scanning technology. Big tech builds expensive drones that 80 percent of farmers cant afford, I think it's bullshit. So we're building a self-flying drone that scans a field in an afternoon and tells farmers what needs attention. currently ag drones cost tens of thousands or more so we're aiming for a fraction of that.

Also an aerodynamics member at Formula UBC, this year I'm leading side tunnel aero design for Formula UBC's 2027 car.
